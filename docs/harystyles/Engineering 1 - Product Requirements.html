Product requirements & business rules

What the system must do, who may do it, what must never happen, and how exceptions behave. Every other part of this handbook implements this one.

Owner Product lead + Tech leadDefines BR-* rules · INV-* invariants · EX-* exceptions · FX-* interactionsAssumes Business Brief v1.0, decisions D1–D12 at their defaults

How to use these IDs

Every rule has a stable ID. Code that implements a rule references it in a comment, and tests reference it in their name, e.g. describe("BR-RA-05 ration composition"). If the code and this document disagree, one of them is wrong: fix both in the same pull request. MUST, SHOULD and MAY follow RFC 2119. Rule IDs are never reused. Retired rules are struck through, not deleted.

1.1 Ubiquitous language

These words mean exactly one thing in code, the database, the API, the UI and conversation. Using a synonym in code (post, subscription, like, creator) is a review blocker. The only exception is inside third-party adapter code, e.g. stripe_subscription_id in the billing infrastructure layer.

WriterA reader with an approved writer profile. Publishes under a pen name.

1.3 Business rules

Identity & accounts (BR-ID)

IDRule
BR-ID-01Sign-in methods: Apple, Google, email magic link, passkey. No passwords.
BR-ID-02One account per verified email (case-insensitive). A new provider identity with a matching verified email is linked, never duplicated. An unverified email never links.
BR-ID-03Readers attest they are 16+ at sign-up. Writers MUST be 18+, verified through payout onboarding before their first payout.
BR-ID-04Account deletion: the account is immediately signed out everywhere, hidden and stops billing. After a 30-day grace period it is hard-deleted or anonymized (§3.6 of Part 3). The user can cancel the deletion during the grace period by signing in.
BR-ID-05Changing email, deleting the account, and staff actions require a recent (≤10 min) re-authentication.
BR-ID-06Display name is optional. Readers appear to writers as their display name, or "A reader" if they have none, unless they are an anonymous patron (BR-BI-12).

Writer lifecycle (BR-WR)

IDRule
BR-WR-01Writer states: applied → approved | waitlisted | declined; approved → suspended → approved; approved|suspended → banned; approved → departed. No other transitions.
BR-WR-02Only approved writers may publish, receive new patronages, or appear in issues.
BR-WR-03Pen name: 2–40 characters. It must be unique after normalization (NFKC, lowercase, collapsed whitespace, diacritics folded). It may change at most once per 90 days. Every old slug redirects permanently.
BR-WR-04A pen name MUST NOT impersonate a real, identifiable person or another writer. Curators enforce this at approval; moderators enforce it on report.
BR-WR-05The writer's legal identity lives only with the payment provider. Our system stores only the provider account ID and the payouts_enabled flag.
BR-WR-06Applications are reviewed within 5 working days. A declined applicant may re-apply after 60 days.
BR-WR-07A writer who departs keeps their data export. Their public letters stay readable for 30 days, then are withdrawn unless the writer chose "leave my letters up".

Letters & notebooks (BR-LE)

IDRule
BR-LE-01Letter states: draft → scheduled → published; draft → published; published → withdrawn (by the writer); published|withdrawn → removed (by a moderator); removed → published (only through an appeal being granted).
BR-LE-02To publish, a letter needs: a title (1–140 characters), a primary mood, a visibility, and a body of 150–20,000 words. A subtitle is optional (≤200 characters).
BR-LE-03Every save creates an immutable revision. The published revision stays fixed until the writer republishes. Readers only ever see the published revision.
BR-LE-04Reading time = ⌈word count ÷ 230⌉ minutes, minimum 1. It is computed on the server at publish.
BR-LE-05Visibility patron: non-entitled viewers get the title, subtitle, content notes and a preview. The preview is the first preview_percent of the words (writer setting 0–30, default 30), capped at 400 words and cut at the end of a sentence. It is always generated on the server.
BR-LE-06Changing visibility from patron to public is always allowed. Changing from public to patron is blocked while the letter is in a scheduled, locked or live issue (error CONTENT_IN_ACTIVE_ISSUE).
BR-LE-07Scheduling: between 10 minutes and 60 days ahead. A scheduled letter publishes within 60 seconds of its time.
BR-LE-08Content notes come from a fixed list: grief, self_harm, suicide, abuse, addiction, illness, violence, sexual_content. Writers set them; curators may add notes but never remove a writer's note.
BR-LE-09A letter belongs to at most one notebook. Positions inside a notebook are unique and contiguous. Deleting a notebook unassigns its letters; it never deletes them.
BR-LE-10Withdrawing is the writer's "delete". The letter disappears from every reader surface. Kept-line snapshots and private messages remain with their owners.
BR-LE-11AI assistance must be disclosed with a checkbox, shown as "AI-assisted" under the title. Fully generated letters are prohibited (moderation reason ai_generated).
BR-LE-12An inbox letter (deliver_to_inbox) MUST have visibility patron. A writer may send at most 4 per calendar month.

Curation & issues (BR-CU)

IDRule
BR-CU-01One issue per (evening date, mood). Exactly 5 slots. Issue states: draft → scheduled → locked → live → archived.
BR-CU-02Eligible letters: published, visibility public, writer approved, not removed, and mood confirmed by a curator.
BR-CU-03Writers in an issue must all be different. A writer may appear at most 2 times per mood in any rolling 7 days (the tool warns at 2 and hard-blocks at 3).
BR-CU-04An issue locks at evening date 00:00 in UTC+14 minus 2 hours, i.e. before the earliest lamp time anywhere on Earth. Once locked, changes are "hot swaps": they need a reason, and they apply only to rations not yet built.
BR-CU-05If no issue is scheduled for a (date, mood) at lock time, the system auto-builds one from the evergreen pool (source = evergreen), following BR-CU-03. Each mood's pool MUST hold at least 30 letters; the dashboard alerts below 40.
BR-CU-06Placement can't be bought, requested or influenced by payments. Curators MUST NOT place their own letters.
BR-CU-07A letter appears in at most one issue per mood per 90 days.

Ration & Tonight (BR-RA)

IDRule
BR-RA-01Lamp time: in sunset mode, local sunset computed from the reader's coarse location cell, clamped to 17:00–21:30 local. Without a location, 18:00 local. In fixed mode, the reader's chosen time (between 15:00 and 23:30).
BR-RA-02Evening date: if local now ≥ today's lamp time, it is today's local date; otherwise yesterday's. The reader's IANA time zone is the only source of local time.
BR-RA-03At most one ration per (reader, evening date). Concurrent requests MUST converge on the same ration.
BR-RA-04A ration is built lazily on the first request after lamp time, or eagerly by the lamp job for readers who opted into the lamp notification.
BR-RA-05Composition: up to 2 personal slots (patron writers first, then followed writers) from letters published in the last 48h. The remaining slots come from the issue for the reader's mood, in curated order. Backfill from the evergreen pool if needed. Constraints: 5 distinct writers; no letter the reader has already read (completed) before; no blocked writer; no withdrawn or removed letter; personal slots may be patron letters only if the reader is entitled to them.
BR-RA-06A built ration is a snapshot. It changes only through: (a) mood switch (BR-RA-07); (b) a letter withdrawn or removed: if the slot is unread it is replaced, if read it becomes a tombstone ("withdrawn by the writer"); (c) the reader blocks a writer: the slot is replaced.
BR-RA-07Mood switch: at most once per ration. Only unread slots are rebuilt, from the new mood's issue. Read slots stay.
BR-RA-08A slot counts as read when scroll progress ≥ 0.8 or active dwell ≥ 50% of reading time. Both are measured on the client, validated for plausibility on the server (dwell can't exceed wall-clock time between heartbeats), and recorded idempotently.
BR-RA-09Before the reader's first lamp time ever, or between rations, Tonight shows the most recent ration (or today's default-mood issue for new readers) plus the countdown to the next lamp time.
BR-RA-10Anonymous visitors get the current issue for the mood they pick in-session (default tender), public letters only, with no personal slots. Nothing is stored on the server.
BR-RA-11The archive is reachable only on purpose: writer pages, notebooks, search, the Shelf. There is no global chronological or popularity list.

Engagement (BR-EN)

IDRule
BR-EN-01Reactions: one per (reader, letter, anchor). The glyph can be changed or removed. Writers can't react to their own letters.
BR-EN-02Reaction totals, kept counts, read counts, follower counts and patron counts are visible only to the letter's writer and to staff. Never to readers. Never in public HTML.
BR-EN-03Kept lines: 1–500 characters, from the published revision, stored with a snapshot of the text, title and pen name. The reader can delete them. They survive later edits, withdrawal or removal of the letter (only the reader sees them).
BR-EN-04Private replies: 1–1,000 characters. A reader may send at most 3 per writer per day and 20 per day in total. Writers reply at most once per reader message. Readers can't message writers who blocked them.
BR-EN-05Yearly letter: an active patron with ≥30 days of tenure may send one yearly message (≤3,000 characters) per writer per 365 days.
BR-EN-06Follow is free, capped at 1,000 writers per reader. Follow lists are private.
BR-EN-07Blocking a writer hides them from every surface for that reader, prevents messages both ways, and does not cancel a patronage (the reader is prompted to cancel).
BR-EN-08Cards render on the server only: quote, pen name, letter title, harystyles mark, in the reader's chosen theme. Cards from patron letters are allowed (the reader is entitled) but limited to the 500-character kept line.

Patronage, billing & tips (BR-BI)

IDRule
BR-BI-01Prices come from the server-side price_points table, per currency and interval. MVP: USD 400/month and 4000/year (minor units), plus GBP, CAD and AUD equivalents. Clients never send amounts for patronage.
BR-BI-02At most one non-ended patronage per (reader, writer). A reader can't be their own patron (same user, or same payment-method fingerprint as the writer's account).
BR-BI-03Bundled billing: each reader has at most one monthly and one yearly billing bundle. All of the reader's patronages on that interval are line items on one charge per period.
BR-BI-04The first patronage in a bundle is charged immediately; access starts only once payment succeeds. Later patronages join the bundle: access starts immediately and the prorated amount goes on the next charge. A reader with no successful charge in their history may have at most 3 unpaid joins.
BR-BI-05Cancelling takes effect at the end of the period, and access lasts until then. Re-starting before the period ends simply undoes the cancellation; there is no new charge.
BR-BI-06Failed renewal: the patronage becomes past_due and access continues for a 3-day grace period. Payment is retried 3 times over 7 days. If all retries fail, the patronage becomes lapsed and access ends. Paying the open invoice within 30 days restores it.
BR-BI-07Tips: 200–20,000 minor units. Tips ≥500 are charged immediately. Tips under 500 require an active bundle and are added to its next charge; otherwise the request is rejected with BILLING_TIP_TOO_SMALL_STANDALONE.
BR-BI-08Writer suspended: their patronage line items are paused (no charge), with a prorated credit for the unused paid time. Reinstated: items resume on the next period. Banned or departed: patronages are cancelled, with a prorated refund to the original payment method.
BR-BI-09Self-serve refund: a reader may get the full amount of their most recent charge back if they ask within 14 days, at most once per 12 months. Access covered by the refunded lines ends immediately. Other refunds go through Finance.
BR-BI-10Mobile apps open checkout on the web where store rules allow. Where they don't, the app shows "Support on the web" with no price and no call to action, as the store guidelines require. In-app purchase is a future entitlement source (EX-14).
BR-BI-11Tax is calculated by the payment provider's tax service. Displayed prices include tax where the law requires it (UK and AU), and exclude it otherwise.
BR-BI-12Anonymous patron: the writer sees "An anonymous patron", and replies are disabled for that patron unless they message first.

Ledger & payouts (BR-LD)

IDRule
BR-LD-01All money is an integer number of minor units plus an ISO currency. Floating point is forbidden anywhere money is handled.
BR-LD-02Split per charge line: net = gross − allocated processor fee − tax; platform = round_half_even(net × 0.10); writer = net − platform. Processor fees are allocated across lines by the largest-remainder method, so the line allocations always add up to the actual fee.
BR-LD-03Every money movement posts one balanced ledger transaction (entries sum to 0 per currency), with an idempotency key derived from the source event.
BR-LD-04Writer funds become available 7 days after the charge succeeds (settlement hold).
BR-LD-05Payout run: the 5th of each month at 09:00 UTC. Pays every writer whose available balance is ≥1,000 minor units and who has payouts_enabled. One payout per (writer, period, currency).
BR-LD-06Refunds and chargebacks after a payout create a negative writer balance. It is recovered from future earnings and never charged to the writer. Dispute fees are absorbed by the platform.
BR-LD-07Writers can download monthly and yearly statements generated from the ledger. Studio numbers come from the ledger, never from separate counters.

Notifications (BR-NO)

IDRule
BR-NO-01Allowed reader push notifications: lamp_lit (off by default), new_letter_from_patronized_writer, private_reply, inbox_letter, billing_problem, security. Nothing else ships without a rule change here.
BR-NO-02Quiet hours (default 23:00–08:00 local): no pushes except security. Deferred pushes are sent at the end of quiet hours, or dropped if they have expired.
BR-NO-03At most 1 push per writer per reader per day, and 3 non-security pushes per reader per day in total.
BR-NO-04Notification text MUST NOT include patron-only body text. Titles are allowed.
BR-NO-05Email is used for receipts, security, inbox letters (if the reader opts in, which is the default) and writer payout notices. One-click unsubscribe is required on all non-transactional email.

Moderation & safety (BR-MO)

IDRule
BR-MO-01Report reasons: harassment, hate, csam, self_harm_promotion, imminent_risk, plagiarism, impersonation, ai_generated, spam, other.
BR-MO-02Priority: csam and imminent_risk are P0 (target: acknowledged within 1 hour, around the clock). Harassment and hate are P1 (24 hours). Everything else is P2 (72 hours).
BR-MO-03CSAM: immediate automatic hiding, evidence preserved under restricted access, legal reporting obligations followed (counsel-defined runbook). Never restored.
BR-MO-04Letters with a self_harm or suicide content note show an interstitial with local crisis resources before the body, once per reader per letter.
BR-MO-05Private messages are screened by a classifier for imminent risk and harassment. Flags create P0/P1 reports. Moderators can read only messages attached to a report.
BR-MO-06Sanctions: warn, remove content, suspend (7, 30 or 90 days), ban. Every sanction notifies the user with a reason and an appeal link. Appeals are accepted within 14 days and reviewed by a different moderator.
BR-MO-07A letter reported by ≥5 distinct readers within 24h is auto-hidden pending review, unless the writer has ≥10 published letters and none removed (brigading protection). In that case it gets priority review instead.

Privacy & preferences (BR-PR)

IDRule
BR-PR-01Reading history, reactions, kept lines, follows and blocks belong to the reader. They are never shown to other readers, sold, or used to train models. They are used only for the reader's own features and for anonymous, aggregated writer stats.
BR-PR-02Location is stored only as a coarse cell (geohash precision 4, roughly 20 km) for sunset calculation. The reader can clear it, which reverts lamp time to 18:00.
BR-PR-03Account-level display preferences: theme ∈ {system, ink, dusk, paper}, theme_auto_by_time, type_pair ∈ {serif_sans, serif_mono, all_serif}, reading_size ∈ {s, m, l}, dyslexia_font. They sync to every client. A device MAY keep a local "this device only" override, which never goes to the server.
BR-PR-04Conflicting preference edits from two devices resolve per field: the latest server-received write wins.
BR-PR-05Emails always render in the paper theme (email client limits). Cards and OG images use the requester's theme, or ink when anonymous.
BR-PR-06Data export (JSON plus a human-readable PDF of the Shelf) is delivered within 72 hours through a signed link valid for 7 days.

1.4 Invariants — what must never happen

Invariants are stronger than rules. Each one has at least one automated test, and where possible a database constraint or runtime assertion that pages on-call if it is violated in production.

1.5 Exceptions & edge cases

IDSituationRequired behavior
EX-01Reader changes time zone (travel)The current ration is kept until the next lamp time computed in the new zone. If that moment is already past, the next request builds the new evening's ration. Never two rations for the same evening date.
EX-02DST transition dayLamp time is computed on the local wall clock for that date. A nonexistent local time moves forward to the first valid minute; an ambiguous time uses the first occurrence.
EX-03Polar day or night (no sunset)Clamp to 17:00–21:30 (BR-RA-01).
EX-04Issue for the reader's mood has fewer than 5 eligible letters for them (all already read)Backfill from the evergreen pool for that mood, then from adjacent moods (fixed adjacency table). If still short, return fewer slots plus the flag short_ration=true, never a duplicate.
EX-05A letter in a live issue is withdrawn or removedEvent → slots replaced or tombstoned in already-built rations (async, ≤5 min). Rations not yet built use the next eligible letter (hot-swap logic).
EX-06Payment webhooks arrive out of order or duplicatedThe inbox processes per customer in provider-timestamp order. State changes are applied only if newer than the stored provider version. Duplicates are ignored.
EX-07Payment provider unavailable during checkoutThe user sees "Payments are taking a moment. Nothing was charged." The patronage stays incomplete and is cleaned up after 24h.
EX-08Writer's payout account is restrictedThe payout is skipped and the balance carries over. The writer gets a notice with the provider's link to fix it.
EX-09Writer withdraws a letter while a reader has it openThe next API call returns 410 CONTENT_WITHDRAWN. Clients show "This letter was withdrawn by the writer." The kept-line snapshot stays.
EX-10Reader edits preferences offline on two devicesPer-field last-write-wins by server receive time (BR-PR-04). The client shows the server value after sync.
EX-11An old mobile app version calls a changed endpointAdditive changes only within /v1. Below the minimum supported version, the API returns 426 with an upgrade link.
EX-12An edit breaks sentence anchorsRe-anchor algorithm (Part 3 §3.9a). Unmatched anchors become orphans: writer totals keep them, highlights hide them, kept lines are unaffected.
EX-13Reader requests account deletion with active patronages or open reportsPatronages are cancelled immediately (no refund unless BR-BI-09 applies). Reports keep pseudonymized reporter and target references until resolved.
EX-14In-app purchase added laterA new entitlement source apple|google. Access checks are unchanged. Store-specific state lives only in the billing infrastructure layer.
EX-15Curator hot-swaps after rations were already builtBuilt rations are untouched (snapshot). The change applies only to rations built afterwards. The audit log records the reason.
